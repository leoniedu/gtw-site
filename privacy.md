# GtW — Grease the Wheel: Privacy Policy

*Effective: November 20, 2026*

GtW is built so that your data never reaches us. We have no servers, no user
accounts, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. We cannot see your data, and
we don't want to.

## What the app stores, and where

**Health data.** The Apple Watch app records each exercise set as a workout in
Apple Health (HealthKit), and reads back your own GtW workouts to show your
daily count and history. By default ("Combine sets daily" in the watch app's
Settings) it then folds a day's sets into one GtW workout per day: it saves a
daily workout holding every set's time and the calories your Apple Watch
measured during them, and only after that is saved removes the single-set
workouts GtW wrote. GtW can only ever change or remove workouts it wrote
itself; Apple Health does not let it touch anything else. The iPhone app reads (never writes) those same
workouts to show your stats. This data lives in your Apple Health database on
your devices, under Apple's Health privacy protections, and syncs between your
devices only through your own iCloud if you have Health syncing enabled. It is
never transmitted to us or to anyone else by GtW.

**Settings.** Your preferences (daily goal, exercise, set duration, reminder
window) are stored on your watch in the app's private storage. They never
leave your device.

**Notifications.** Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your watch.
No notification service, no push servers — nothing is sent anywhere.

## What we collect

Nothing. GtW makes no network connections. There is no account to create, no
identifier we hold, no analytics event, no crash-reporting SDK, and no
advertising.

## Health data commitments

As required by Apple's guidelines, and as a matter of principle: GtW never
uses Health data for advertising or marketing, never sells or shares it, and
never writes false data to HealthKit. Every set GtW records is one you
actually performed during a live workout session; the daily workout only
combines those recorded sets and adds no time or calories of its own.

## Your control

You can revoke GtW's Health access at any time in the Health app (Sharing →
Apps → GtW), and delete any or all GtW workouts in the Health app. Deleting
the app deletes its settings.

## Children

GtW is not directed at children under 13.
