GtW — Grease the Wheel: Support

Contact: gtw@livehardr.com — I read everything. Include your watchOS/iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

Common questions

Why don't I see my sets in the iPhone app? HealthKit syncs from watch to iPhone with a short delay — a just-finished set can take a few minutes to appear. Pull down to refresh. If nothing ever appears, check the Health app → Profile → Apps → GtW and make sure workout reading is allowed.

Why did my reminder not arrive? Check three things: notifications are allowed for GtW (watch Settings → Notifications), Time Sensitive notifications are enabled for GtW, and your active Focus/Sleep mode allows GtW notifications. The app's Settings screen shows the next queued nudge time.

Does GtW really count toward the Exercise ring? Yes — that's the point. Each set runs a real live workout session on the watch for the duration of the set, which is what earns ring credit. A set of 30 seconds or more is reliably credited; shorter sets depend on your measured effort.

Why do I see one GtW workout per day in Fitness? Ring credit requires a live workout session per set, so each set is first saved as its own workout. GtW then combines the day's sets into one daily workout so Fitness isn't flooded with tiny entries; its time and calories are the sum of the sets. Prefer one entry per set? Turn off "Combine sets daily" in the watch app's Settings.

How do I stop reminders? From any nudge: "Stop nudges for today" (resumes tomorrow) or "Turn off nudges" (stays off). Both also live in the watch app's Settings.
